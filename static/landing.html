How it works

From a passing thought to a verdict

No forms, no journaling. You just talk. Sabha turns weeks of your own words into a council that knows what you actually care about.

1 · You talk

Speak your goals, fears and plans into the Omi app. Live transcripts stream to Sabha over webhooks.

Omi

2 · It remembers

Each sentence is redacted, labelled as a goal, value, fear, plan, fact or past decision, embedded, and stored as a memory.

Qdrant

3 · The council debates

“Sabha, should I…?” Five advisors cite your dated memories, answer each other, and get audited for bias.

Lyzr agents

4 · Verdict & learning

A weighted vote becomes a verdict and an action item in your Omi app. Tell it how things went, and the right advisors gain power.

Omi + Qdrant
The council

Five voices. One of them is you.

Each advisor is its own Lyzr agent with its own persona and its own slice of your memories. Every claim must cite one of your memories, or be marked a guess.

Features

Built for real decisions

Everything runs on your own words, and the council gets wiser every time you report back.

Memory Constellation new

Your Qdrant memory vectors in 3D. Memories the advisors cite light up as they speak.

Tipping Point new

Every verdict shows what would have flipped it: which advisor switching sides, or how much more weight a dissenter needed.

Omi Day Summary new

Omi's nightly recap flows in automatically. Decisions become past decisions, and open questions become plans.

Outcome learning

“Sabha, the startup decision went well.” Advisors who called it gain vote weight, and you can see the weights change.

Interrupt by voice

“Sabha, I also care about…” mid-debate. It's saved as a value, and round two must weigh it.

Verdict back to Omi

The verdict becomes an Omi memory, and the next step lands in your Omi action items.

Privacy

Your words, your rules

A decision engine is only useful if you can be honest with it.

Sealed memories“Sabha, seal that.” Advisors may reason with a sealed memory but can never quote it, and a server-side check enforces that.
Off the recordNothing is stored until you say “back on”.
Redacted at the doorPhone numbers and emails become [REDACTED] before anything is embedded or stored.
Local embeddingsVectors are computed on your machine. Only the memories relevant to a question are sent to the council.
